As you can see , i couldn't figure that out, but i found out something else. 
Instead of using count i could use the `measure` keyword to get the count as well!!! 

```powershell
PS C:\users\century8\desktop> Get-Content .\unique.txt | Sort-Object | Get-Unique -AsString | measure                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                           
Count    : 696

```
