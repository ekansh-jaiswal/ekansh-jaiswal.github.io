Here When we use `Select-Object -Property Description` without the `-Properties` parameter, we are telling PowerShell: "Look at the data you already downloaded and show me the Description column." 
Because `Get-ADComputer` didn't download that data in the first place, the column appears empty.
Adding `-Properties Description` forces PowerShell to actually request that specific information from the Active Directory server.
